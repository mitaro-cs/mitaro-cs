Material in the profile generator:

- `metrics.json`: advance widths of Liberation Sans (SIL Open Font License 1.1), used only to measure text.

No font is embedded; the SVGs use the system sans-serif font.
- `land.txt`: land and sea on a 2° grid, rasterized from Natural Earth `ne_110m_land` (public domain,
  naturalearthdata.com), used to draw the ASCII Earth on the cover.
- `icons.json`: logo paths from Simple Icons (CC0 1.0, simpleicons.org) for the tech stack and social badges.
  The logos stay the trademarks of their owners.
